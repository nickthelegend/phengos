Phengos ships no binary art: lanterns, halos, rings, the paper room, outlines and stars are generated in code
(`packages/app/src/game/visuals.ts`, `room-system.ts`, `contrast-system.ts`). Hums and chimes are synthesised
with Web Audio at runtime (`audio-system.ts`). Nothing below is a brand, logo or recognisable product.
