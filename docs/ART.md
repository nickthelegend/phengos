# phengos art direction

Generated with ChatGPT's image model (via the Codex image tool on a ChatGPT plan). Source PNGs are kept out of git; the app ships WebP copies.

- **Style and palette:** warm night watercolour with soft glow, palette deep blue night #1d2a4a, warm lamp gold #ffd27a, paper white #fbf6ea, rose #f2a6a0.
- **Banned:** real brands or logos, readable text, real people, other designers' artwork.
- **Files:** `packages/app/public/art/`
