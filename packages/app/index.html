PHENGOS / A GAME OF HIDING LIGHTS

Catch the light

Small paper lights hide behind your real furniture. Every way to play is a full way to play.

  • Point with your hand ray and pinch
  • Look and hold (head on Quest, eyes on Meta VR Glasses)
  • One switch: any pinch catches the outlined light
  • Voice, optional: "catch", "next", "hint"
  • Listen: each light hums from where it hides

Seated is fine. Nothing to reach for.
