Catch the light

Twelve paper lights hide in your room. Any one way finishes the game.

Ways to play
  • Point and pinch.
  • Look and hold. Head on Quest, eyes on Glasses.
  • One switch. Any pinch catches the outline.
  • Voice, optional. Nine words, on the device.
  • Or follow the hum if you can't see the light.
