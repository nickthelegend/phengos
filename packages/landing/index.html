φέγγος / glow / Meta Quest, mixed reality

Lights hide in your room. Catch them any way you play.

Small paper lights hide behind your real couch, under your table, in a ceiling corner. Point and pinch, look and hold, say "catch", or press one switch. If you can't see them, follow their hum.

Point →

Aim your hand ray at a light and pinch. It floats to your palm.

Look ↑

Hold your view on a light for one second. On Quest 3 this is head-gaze, built by us and labelled "Look (head)": Quest 3 has no eye tracking. On Meta VR Glasses it follows your eyes.

One switch ↻

The outline steps from light to light. Any pinch, from either hand, catches the outlined one. A long pinch opens the menu.

Voice, if you like →

Nine words: catch, next, back, hint, repeat, pause, resume, menu, louder. Recognised on your headset by Vosk. No audio is stored or sent. Never required.

Follow the hum ←

Each light hums from where it hides. Higher means nearer the ceiling. The hum pulses faster as you aim closer. Every sound has a caption: "hum, left, low".

High contrast ↓

Your real walls and furniture are redrawn as bold yellow or white outlines on black, and the lights become solid yellow glows. Geometry only: no app can recolour passthrough.

Your room is the level →

Phengos reads the room your headset already scanned: furniture with its labels, walls, ceiling. Each light hides where you can just see part of it from your seat, inside a comfortable turn. Depth occlusion makes it really disappear behind your couch. Three rounds a night: 5, 8 and 12 lights. Every light you catch rises to your ceiling and joins a constellation that grows night after night.

What is tested, and what is not yet ↓

Tested in the Meta Immersive Web Emulator. Not yet measured on a headset.
